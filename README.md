# Super-Sales-Dashboard
OBJECTIVE To contribute to the success of a business by utilizing data analysis techniques, specifically focusing on time series analysis, to provide valuable insights and accurate sales forecasting.

* Developed an interactive **Super Sales Dashboard using Power BI** to analyze sales performance, revenue, profit, and business trends.
* Created **KPI cards and interactive visuals** to track key metrics and monitor overall sales performance.
* Analyzed sales by **region, category, product, and time period** to identify top-performing areas and products.
* Used **Power BI, Power Query, and DAX** to transform data, create calculated measures, and generate actionable business insights.
